Not just reminders. An agent that follows the work through.

Traditional automation executes a trigger. TabBeagle is being designed as an agentic operating layer for AR: it observes what changed, reasons over context, takes an authorized action, and then evaluates what happened next.

01 · OBSERVE
See what changedTrack invoices, aging, replies, promises to pay, payment events and exceptions.
02 · REASON
Choose the next moveUse invoice and customer context, timing, history and your approved playbooks to determine the next action.
03 · ACT + FOLLOW THROUGH
Execute, then check the outcomeRecommend or execute the approved follow-up, then re-check until payment, resolution or human escalation.

The Agentic AR loop.

The system is designed to keep working on the case instead of stopping after a single email or reminder.

Observe→Reason→Act→Check result→Repeat / Escalate

Automation is not the same as Agentic AI.

This distinction matters because businesses do not need another tool that only fires scheduled reminders. TabBeagle is intended to manage the next decision in the workflow, not simply send the next message.

TRADITIONAL AUTOMATION
Fixed trigger → fixed action
IF invoice_overdue > 7 days
THEN send_email(template_2)
  • Executes a predefined rule.
  • Limited awareness of what happened before or after.
  • Usually waits for a person to decide the next step.
TABBEAGLE AGENTIC AI
Observe → Reason → Act → Check result
CONTEXT → NEXT AUTHORIZED ACTION → OUTCOME → RE-EVALUATE / ESCALATE
  • Uses invoice and customer context to prioritize the case.
  • Selects from authorized playbooks instead of blindly repeating reminders.
  • Tracks promises, replies and payment state before acting again.
  • Escalates exceptions when human judgment is needed.
Example: what “follow through” means
DAY 12

Invoice is overdue. The agent reviews the account and recent activity.

CONTEXT

The customer already promised payment Friday, so duplicate outreach is paused.

FRIDAY

No payment arrives. The agent selects the next authorized escalation.

OUTCOME

Payment detected → case closes. Exception → human review is requested.

Controlled autonomy
Recommend → Approve → Automate.

Agentic does not mean uncontrolled. TabBeagle is being designed so businesses can begin with recommendations and approvals, then grant more autonomy only to trusted, repeatable actions.

Visibility
Know what the agent recommends, what it did and what changed.
Control
Pause follow-up, approve sensitive actions or take over a customer conversation.
Boundaries
Define tone, timing, escalation rules and which actions may run automatically.
History
Keep a clear activity trail around invoices, follow-up, promises and outcomes.
