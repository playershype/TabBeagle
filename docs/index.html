THE AGENTIC LOOP

Not just reminders. An agent that follows the work through.

The difference is what happens after a trigger. TabBeagle is designed to continuously observe the state of receivables, reason over context, act inside your permissions, and evaluate what happened next.

01 · OBSERVE

See what changed

Invoices, aging, replies, promises to pay, payment events and exceptions.

02 · REASON

Choose the next move

Evaluate context, history, priority, timing and the playbook you have authorized.

03 · ACT

Execute the work

Recommend or carry out the next approved follow-up, pause, reschedule or escalation.

04 · FOLLOW THROUGH

Check the outcome

Re-evaluate the case after the action and keep going until payment, resolution or human escalation.

AUTOMATION ≠ AGENTIC AI

A rule sends an email. An agent manages the next decision.

Traditional automation

IF invoice_overdue > 7 days
THEN send_email(template_2)
  • Executes a fixed trigger.
  • Little awareness of what happened before or after.
  • The workflow usually waits for a person to decide the next step.

TabBeagle Agentic AI

OBSERVE → REASON → ACT → CHECK RESULT → REPEAT / ESCALATE
  • Uses invoice and customer context to prioritize the case.
  • Selects the next action from authorized playbooks.
  • Tracks promises, responses and payment state before acting again.
  • Escalates exceptions instead of blindly repeating reminders.
