## 1. What this policy covers
This policy explains what information the TabBeagle app and website ("Service") collect when a business or freelancer uses the Service to track customers, invoices, and payment reminders.

## 2. Information we collect
- **Account information:** your work email address and the authentication records needed to sign you in.
- **Business information you enter:** organization name, and the customers and invoices you create.
- **Customer information you enter:** customer name, company name, contact name, phone number, email address, billing and work addresses, payment terms, reminder preferences, notes, and tags. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to store this information about your customers.
- **Usage and technical information:** basic device and app information, errors, and timestamps needed to keep the Service working and secure.
- **Audit records:** records of important actions (for example, creating, editing, or archiving a customer) to protect your data and support disputes.

We do not sell personal information.

## 3. How we use information
- To provide the Service: sign-in, storing your customers and invoices, showing balances and overdue status.
- To send messages you choose to send, such as payment reminders, to the recipients you enter.
- To secure the Service, prevent fraud and abuse, and fix errors.
- To communicate with you about your account.
