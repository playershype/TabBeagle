Reminders vs. a controlled agent

A reminder fires on a date. An agent looks at the case first.

Scheduled reminders are useful, and many tools already send them. The difference we are building is what happens when a customer has already replied, promised to pay, or disputed the invoice.

Scheduled reminders

IF invoice_overdue > 7 days
THEN send_email(template_2)
  • Fire on a date, whatever happened since.
  • Don't know that a customer already promised to pay.
  • Send the same template again and again.
  • Leave every next decision to you.

TabBeagle agent In development

OBSERVE → REASON → RECOMMEND → APPROVE → FOLLOW THROUGH
  • Reads the invoice, the history and any promise to pay before proposing anything.
  • Chooses from playbooks you approve, including how firm the tone may be.
  • Waits on purpose when waiting is the better move.
  • Stops and asks you when a case looks unusual.
Human control

Recommend. Approve. Then automate what you trust.

An agent that talks to your customers needs boundaries. These are the controls we are designing into the product from the start.

  • Set the rules

    Choose the tone, timing limits and which playbooks the agent may use.

  • Approve before anything goes out

    Customer messages and changes to an invoice wait for your sign-off until you choose to relax that rule for a specific, repeatable action.

  • Pause or take over

    Stop follow-up on any customer, or pick up the conversation yourself.

  • See every action

    An activity trail records what was proposed, who approved it, what was sent and what changed afterwards.

These are the design commitments for the agent. For what works in the test build today, see the FAQ.

FAQ

Straight answers

Is the AI agent available today?

Not yet. The current test build lets an organization track customers, invoices and aging, with each organization's data kept separate. The agent that prioritizes cases, recommends next steps and follows up is in development.

Will TabBeagle message my customers on its own?

Not in the current build. Once follow-up is available, it is designed to run only inside playbooks you have approved, and you can pause it at any time. Messages that need your judgment wait for your approval.

Does it connect to QuickBooks, Stripe or my bank?

No integration is live yet. QuickBooks, Stripe and bank connections are planned. We will not describe any connection as working until it has been tested end to end.

How is my data protected?

Each organization sees only its own records, and the database enforces that separation. We have not yet completed an independent security audit or any compliance certification, and this site makes no SOC 2 or similar claim.

What happens to the details I submit?

They are used only to contact you about early access. A full privacy policy will be published before early access opens. You can ask us to remove your details at any time.

How much will it cost?

Pricing is still being finalized. Early access members will hear about it first.
